Date: 2026-10-24
  Changes:
    - Factorio 2.1: fluid connections read the new way, recycling recipes found by their category list.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-16
  Features:
    - "Pipe choice" setting (per player): same kind as the underground (default), one you have or can
      hand-craft, or researched pipes only. Each puts its rule first and uses the others as tie-breaks
    - A pipe with a different connection category than the underground is never used
  Bugfixes:
    - Recycling recipes no longer count when matching a pipe to its underground
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-10-16
  Features:
    - "Never fill with" setting (map settings): entity names never put into a gap, void-pipe by default.
      The next matching pipe or belt is used instead, or the gap is left empty
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-14
  Features:
    - Fills the one-tile gap after an underground pipe (to another underground or a machine's pipe connection)
      or underground belt (exit to the next entrance, straight or round a corner)
    - Waits until you stop building, so drag-built undergrounds keep their full range
    - Uses the matching pipe or belt and the underground's quality, from your inventory or as a ghost
